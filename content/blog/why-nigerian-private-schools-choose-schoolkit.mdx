Running a private school in Nigeria means juggling fee collection, attendance,
results, and parent communication — often across a messy mix of Excel sheets,
WhatsApp groups, and paper files. SchoolKit was built to bring all of that into
one place, designed specifically for how Nigerian schools actually operate.

## What SchoolKit Does

SchoolKit is an all-in-one school management platform covering the day-to-day
work of running a school:

## Why SchoolKit, and Not the Alternatives

**Built for Nigeria, not adapted for it.** Most school management software is
built for schools in the US or Europe and retrofitted for local use. SchoolKit
starts from Nigerian realities: Paystack for payments, WhatsApp/SMS for parent
updates, and offline support for when the network isn't cooperating.

**Real payments, not just invoicing.** Many platforms will generate an invoice
but leave the actual collection process to you. SchoolKit closes that loop —
parents pay directly through Paystack, and your fee records update
automatically.

**Works when the internet doesn't.** Offline functionality means attendance,
results entry, and other daily tasks don't grind to a halt during a network
outage — a genuine day-to-day reality for many schools.
