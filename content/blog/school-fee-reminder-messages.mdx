Chasing school fees is the job nobody at a school enjoys. The bursar doesn't want to sound rude; the proprietor needs the money to pay salaries; and parents who *are* trying to pay feel embarrassed every time a reminder arrives.

The right message, sent at the right time, fixes most of that. Below are **25 ready-to-send school fee reminder messages** — for WhatsApp and SMS, from a friendly early reminder to a final notice — that you can copy, fill in and send.

Replace anything in [square brackets] before sending.

## The Golden Rules of Fee Reminders

1. **Always private.** Never name a parent or child who owes fees in a class group or on a notice board. It humiliates families and can breach Nigeria's data protection rules.
2. **Always specific.** Child's name, class, amount, due date, how to pay. Vague messages get vague responses.
3. **Always accurate.** Check the balance before you send. Chasing a parent who has already paid does more damage than not reminding at all.
4. **Escalate gently.** Friendly → firm → final, a week apart, with a phone call before the final notice.
5. **Thank every payment.** A thank-you message makes the next reminder easier.

## Before Resumption

**1. Early reminder (two weeks before resumption)**

> Good morning [Parent name]. We hope you're enjoying the holiday. [School name] resumes on [date]. [Child's name]'s fees for [term] are ₦[amount]. You can pay by [bank transfer to account / online link] before resumption. Thank you for your continued support.

**2. Fee schedule notice**

> Dear [Parent name], please find [Child's name]'s [term] fee schedule for [class]: Tuition ₦[x], [levy] ₦[x], [item] ₦[x]. Total: ₦[total]. Payment is due by [date]. Kindly reach out if you'd like to discuss an instalment plan.

**3. Instalment plan offer**

> Dear [Parent name], to make planning easier, [School name] accepts [term] fees in [2/3] instalments: ₦[amount] by [date] and ₦[amount] by [date]. Reply to this message if you would like to use the plan for [Child's name].

## Around the Due Date

**4. One week before**

> Good morning [Parent name]. A gentle reminder that [Child's name]'s fees of ₦[amount] are due on [date]. Pay via [payment method]. Thank you.

**5. On the due date**

> Dear [Parent name], today is the due date for [Child's name]'s [term] fees (₦[amount]). If you have already paid, please accept our thanks and ignore this message. To pay: [payment method].

**6. Bank transfer details**

> Dear [Parent name], [Child's name]'s fees can be paid to: [Bank], [Account name], [Account number]. Please use "[Child's name] – [Class]" as the narration so we can confirm it quickly. Thank you.

## Overdue Balances

**7. Friendly overdue (3–5 days after)**

> Dear [Parent name], we noticed [Child's name]'s [term] fees of ₦[amount] are still outstanding. It may have slipped through — kindly pay at your earliest convenience via [payment method]. Thank you.

**8. Second reminder (one week after)**

> Dear [Parent name], this is a reminder that ₦[amount] remains outstanding on [Child's name]'s [term] fees, which were due on [date]. Please make payment by [new date], or contact the bursary on [phone] to agree a plan.

**9. Firm reminder (two weeks after)**

> Dear [Parent name], [Child's name]'s fees of ₦[amount] are now two weeks overdue. We kindly ask that payment be made by [date]. If there's a difficulty, please call [name] on [phone] — we would rather agree a plan with you than send further reminders.

**10. Invitation to meet**

> Dear [Parent name], we would like to discuss [Child's name]'s outstanding balance of ₦[amount]. Could you visit the school or call [name] on [phone] this week? We're happy to work out a payment arrangement that suits you.

**11. Final notice (after a phone call)**

> Dear [Parent name], following our conversation on [date], this is a final reminder that ₦[amount] is outstanding on [Child's name]'s fees. As agreed, payment is due by [date]. In line with the school's fee policy, [state the consequence your policy sets out]. Thank you for your cooperation.

Only send a final notice with a consequence your written fee policy actually contains, and never involve the child — no announcements in class, no sending pupils home mid-lesson without speaking to the parent first.

## Part Payments

**12. Acknowledging a part payment**

> Thank you, [Parent name]. We've received ₦[amount paid] towards [Child's name]'s [term] fees. The balance is ₦[balance], due by [date]. We appreciate it.

**13. Balance reminder after a part payment**

> Dear [Parent name], a reminder that the remaining balance of ₦[balance] on [Child's name]'s fees is due on [date]. Thank you for the payment already made.

**14. Instalment due**

> Dear [Parent name], [Child's name]'s [second/final] instalment of ₦[amount] is due on [date], as agreed. Pay via [payment method]. Thank you.

## Thank-You and Confirmation Messages

**15. Full payment received**

> Thank you, [Parent name]! [Child's name]'s [term] fees of ₦[amount] have been received in full. Your receipt is attached. We appreciate your prompt payment.

**16. Early payment thank-you**

> Dear [Parent name], thank you for paying [Child's name]'s fees ahead of resumption. It makes a real difference to how we plan the term.

**17. Confirming a transfer**

> Dear [Parent name], we've confirmed your transfer of ₦[amount] for [Child's name] on [date]. Thank you — your receipt will follow shortly.

## Other Situations

**18. Fees for siblings**

> Dear [Parent name], the [term] fees for your children are: [Child 1] ₦[x], [Child 2] ₦[x] (sibling discount applied). Total ₦[total], due by [date]. Thank you.

**19. Exam or result release (where your policy requires fees to be cleared)**

> Dear [Parent name], [term] examinations begin on [date]. Our records show ₦[amount] outstanding on [Child's name]'s fees. Kindly clear it or contact the bursary before [date] so we can discuss it in good time.

**20. New-term fee increase notice**

> Dear [Parent name], from [term/session], [class] fees will be ₦[new amount] per term. This change reflects [brief reason]. We're giving notice now so families can plan ahead. Thank you for your understanding.

## SMS Versions (Under 160 Characters)

**21.** `[School]: [Child]'s fees of N[amount] are due [date]. Pay to [Bank] [Acct no]. Narration: [Child] [Class]. Thank you.`

**22.** `[School]: Reminder - N[amount] outstanding on [Child]'s fees, due [date]. Already paid? Please ignore. Thank you.`

**23.** `[School]: Thank you! We received N[amount] for [Child]. Balance: N[balance], due [date].`

**24.** `[School]: [Child]'s fees are now overdue. Kindly pay N[amount] by [date] or call [phone] to agree a plan.`

**25.** `[School]: Payment of N[amount] for [Child] received in full. Thank you for your support.`

Use "N" rather than "₦" in SMS: the naira sign can turn a 160-character message into two billable messages on some networks.

## When to Send

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| 2 weeks before resumption | Early reminder + fee schedule |
| 1 week before the due date | Gentle reminder |
| Due date | Due-date reminder |
| 3–5 days overdue | Friendly overdue |
| 1 week overdue | Second reminder |
| 2 weeks overdue | Firm reminder + phone call |
| After the call | Final notice, if needed |
| Every payment | Thank-you or part-payment acknowledgement |

Send between 8 a.m. and 7 p.m. — never late at night — and avoid Sunday mornings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you politely remind parents about school fees?

Address the parent by name, state the child's name, class, the amount outstanding and the due date, give the payment options, and close with thanks. Keep the tone factual and private — one parent, one message, never a class group.

### When should schools send fee reminders?

A good rhythm is two weeks before resumption, one week before the due date, on the due date, and then a weekly reminder for overdue balances with a firmer tone each time. Send in the morning or early evening, not late at night.

### Should fee reminders be sent in the class WhatsApp group?

No. Naming parents or children who owe fees in a group embarrasses families, damages trust and can breach Nigeria's data protection rules. Send reminders privately, one parent at a time.

### How long should an SMS fee reminder be?

Under 160 characters, so it sends as a single SMS. Include the child's name, the amount, the due date and how to pay; leave the rest for WhatsApp or a call.
