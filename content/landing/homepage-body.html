Frequently asked questions

How do I collect school fees online in Nigeria?

SchoolKit integrates directly with Paystack — Nigeria's most trusted payment platform. Parents can pay via card, bank transfer or USSD. Payments are tracked in real-time and digital receipts are sent instantly.

Does SchoolKit work without internet?

Yes — SchoolKit is 100% offline-ready. You can mark attendance, record payments and manage student data without internet. Everything syncs automatically when your connection returns.

How much does school management software cost in Nigeria?

SchoolKit starts completely free for schools with up to 100 students. Paid plans start at ₦45,000 per term (₦135,000/year) for up to 200 students — that's less than ₦1,000 per student per year.

Is SchoolKit suitable for small private schools?

Yes — SchoolKit was built specifically for Nigerian private schools of all sizes. The Free plan supports up to 100 students. The Starter plan at ₦45,000/term covers up to 200 students with full features.

Can parents pay school fees on WhatsApp?

Parents receive payment links via WhatsApp that they can pay directly via Paystack. Receipts are also sent to parents on WhatsApp automatically.

How is SchoolKit different from other school management software in Nigeria?

SchoolKit is built from scratch for Nigerian schools — not adapted from a UK or Indian product. It handles the specific realities of Nigerian schools: Paystack payments, WhatsApp receipts, offline mode for when NEPA takes light, and WAEC-aligned grading.
