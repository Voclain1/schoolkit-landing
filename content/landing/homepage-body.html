Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
How do I collect school fees online in Nigeria?

SchoolKit connects fee collection to Paystack. Parents can use supported payment methods such as card, bank transfer or USSD. Payments, balances and digital receipts stay connected to student records.

How much does SchoolKit cost?

The Free plan covers up to 100 students. Starter is ₦45,000 per term for up to 200 students, Growth is ₦90,000 for up to 600, and Professional is ₦180,000 for up to 1,200. Enterprise pricing is quoted per school. Yearly billing covers three terms with a 10% discount. Payment-processing and communication charges should be checked separately.

Is SchoolKit live? Can my school start today?

SchoolKit is live. Sign up your school to get started, or message the team on WhatsApp if you want a walkthrough for your school.

Can SchoolKit handle online admissions?

Yes. Families can apply through your school’s admissions page and upload documents. Staff can review applications, record entrance assessments, send offers and admit accepted applicants without retyping their details.

Does everything work without internet?

SchoolKit includes an offline mode for supported tasks. Online payments, sending messages and syncing records need a connection. Ask for a demonstration of the specific tasks your team needs during an outage.

Can I check which features are included in my plan?

Yes. Review the plan comparison and ask the SchoolKit team to confirm the plan needed for your school’s admissions, store, homework or other operations before subscribing.
