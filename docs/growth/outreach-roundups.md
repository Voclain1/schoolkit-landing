Goal: SchoolKit listed in 15 "best school management software in Nigeria" articles
within 12 months. These lists rank on Google and are what AI assistants read when
asked for a recommendation.

## Email 2: follow-up (7 days later, once only)

**Subject:** Re: An addition for your school management software list

> Hi [Name], just bringing this back to the top of your inbox. Happy to send a demo login or a short write-up in your format if that's easier. Thanks again. [Name]

## What to offer

- A demo account with sample data (set one up before the first pitch).
- A pioneer school willing to take a call (ask first; see the review and case study requests).
- Exact facts from the press page: never a different price or feature list.

## What not to do

- Don't pay for inclusion in an editorial list. If an outlet only lists paying vendors, the placement must be marked sponsored, and it counts as advertising, not a recommendation.
- Don't ask the author to remove or criticise a competitor.
- Don't send more than one follow-up.
