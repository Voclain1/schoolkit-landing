# Partners and press

## Partners (weeks 8–12)

| Partner | Ask | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NAPPS (National Association of Proprietors of Private Schools): state chapters, starting with Lagos, Abuja (FCT), Rivers and Oyo | A 20-minute slot at a chapter meeting: "How to collect 90% of fees by week 3"; a listing on the chapter's site or newsletter | Direct access to proprietors; a trusted, relevant link |
| Paystack | A listing in their partner or customer showcase; a joint guide on school fee collection | High-authority, highly relevant link; credibility with schools |
| School suppliers (uniforms, books, furniture, ICT) | Swap guest articles or resource links; joint resumption-season offers | Same buyers, no competition |
| Education consultants and school-setup advisers | A referral arrangement; a guest guide on starting a private school | They are asked "which software?" every week |

## Press (from month 3)

Pitch stories, not the product. The three angles that work:

1. **Data:** findings from the State of Nigerian Private Schools survey ([school-survey.md](school-survey.md)), such as average fees by state, how late fees really are, and how long results take.
2. **Commentary:** founder comment on school-fee news, naira-driven fee increases, resumption, or new education policy.
3. **Founder story:** why a Nigerian-built product for Nigerian schools.

Outlets to target: Nairametrics, TechCabal, Techpoint Africa, BusinessDay, Punch (Education), The Guardian Nigeria (Education), Legit.ng, Vanguard (Education), Premium Times.
