# Writer brief template

Fill one in per piece before a writer starts. Writers should be Nigerian, ideally
former teachers, bursars or school administrators.

```
Working title:
Target query (one):
Related queries to cover:
Searcher: proprietor / bursar / admin / teacher / parent
What they need to walk away with:
What the top 3 Google results already cover (and what they miss):
Our angle: the thing this page has that they don't (template, worked example, naira figures, Nigerian case)
Must include:
- A direct answer in the first two sentences
- Question-style subheadings
- At least one table or worked example
- Naira figures and dates where relevant ("as of [month year]")
- 4–5 FAQs at the end (also added to the frontmatter `faqs`)
Internal links:
- Feature page: /features/...
- Two related guides: /blog/..., /blog/...
- Tool, if relevant: /tools/...
Length guide: as long as the answer needs, usually 1,200–2,500 words
Facts to verify before publishing:
Reviewer:
Due date:
```

## House style

- Nigerian terms: term (not semester), proprietor, bursar, JSS/SSS, resumption, PTA.
- Naira as ₦45,000; dates as 5 October 2026.
- Short sentences, no filler intros, no "in today's fast-paced world".
- Describe SchoolKit only with features marked "Live now" on the homepage.
- No student names, photos or data. Example names in worked examples are fine.
- AI tools may help with outlines and first drafts; a person with school experience writes the examples and checks every fact.
