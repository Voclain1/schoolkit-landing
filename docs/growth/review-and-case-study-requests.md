# Review and case study requests

Reviews on Google, Capterra and G2 are among the sources AI assistants cite most
when asked "which school software is best". Case studies give buyers, and AI, real
numbers to quote.

## Rules

- Ask every live school, not only the happy ones. Platforms penalise filtered or "gated" review requests.
- **Never pay for reviews or trade them for discounts or free terms.** Google forbids incentivised reviews, and review sites have their own strict rules. The safe default is to offer nothing; check a platform's current guidelines before running any review campaign.
- Never write a review for a school, and never post one from a SchoolKit staff account.
- Case studies use school-level numbers only, with written permission. No student names or photos.

## When to ask

At day 30 after a school goes live, and again after a visible win: the first term's fees collected, or the first report cards released.

## WhatsApp: review request

> Good morning [Name]. Thank you for using SchoolKit at [School name] this term. Would you share a short review of your experience? It takes two minutes and helps other proprietors decide.
>
> Google: [Google review link]
> Capterra: [Capterra review link]
>
> Honest feedback, good or bad, helps us improve. Thank you!

Get the Google link from Google Business Profile → *Ask for reviews*. Capterra and G2 each give a review-request link once the profile is claimed.

## Email: case study request

**Subject:** Could we share [School name]'s story?

> Dear [Name],
>
> Since [School name] started using SchoolKit, [the specific result you've seen, e.g. "most fees for this term were paid online"]. Other proprietors would find your experience genuinely useful.
>
> Would you be willing to do a 20-minute call for a short case study? You approve every word before it's published. We only use school-level figures, never anything about individual students, and we can leave the school's name out if you prefer.
>
> Thank you,
> [Name]

## Case study interview questions

1. How did the school handle fees, results and attendance before SchoolKit?
2. What was the biggest frustration?
3. Why did you choose SchoolKit, and what else did you consider?
4. How did setup and staff training go?
5. What has changed? Ask for numbers: time spent on results, share of fees paid on time, calls from parents about balances.
6. What would you tell another proprietor considering it?
7. What should SchoolKit improve?

## Case study page structure

Title with the result ("How [School] collected [x]% of fees in the first three weeks of term"); a one-paragraph summary; the before; why SchoolKit; the setup; the results with numbers; a quote; school facts (location, levels, size band).
