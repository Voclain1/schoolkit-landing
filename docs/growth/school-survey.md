# State of Nigerian Private Schools: survey

The annual data report is the strategy's main link-earner and press hook. This
is the questionnaire. Send it to the waitlist, pioneer schools, NAPPS contacts
and proprietor groups in weeks 8–12, then publish in month 4–5.

## Ground rules

- Anonymous by default. Ask for school name and contact only in an optional final question, for people who want the report emailed.
- No student-level data of any kind.
- Report results only in aggregate, and only for groups of at least 10 schools, so no single school can be identified.
- State the sample size, dates and how respondents were recruited in the report.
- Use Google Forms or Typeform; keep it under 7 minutes.

## Questions

**About the school**

1. Which state is the school in? *(dropdown, 36 states + FCT)*
2. Which levels does it run? *(Nursery / Primary / Junior Secondary / Senior Secondary; tick all)*
3. How many students are enrolled? *(1–100 / 101–200 / 201–600 / 601–1,200 / 1,200+)*
4. What is your role? *(Proprietor / Principal or head teacher / Administrator / Bursar / Other)*

**Fees**

5. What is the tuition per term for your most common class? *(under ₦50k / ₦50–100k / ₦100–200k / ₦200–500k / over ₦500k)*
6. Did fees rise for the 2026/2027 session? By roughly how much? *(No / up to 10% / 10–25% / 25–50% / over 50%)*
7. By the end of week 3 of a typical term, what share of fees has been paid? *(under 25% / 25–50% / 50–75% / over 75%)*
8. How do most parents pay? *(Cash at school / Bank transfer / Card or online payment / POS / Mixed)*
9. How do you confirm a bank transfer? *(Bank alert / Parent sends a screenshot / Bank statement at month end / Software matches it / Other)*
10. Roughly how many hours a week does the school spend chasing and confirming fees? *(0–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / over 10)*
11. Do you offer instalment plans? *(Yes, published / Yes, case by case / No)*

**Results**

12. How are results processed? *(By hand / Excel or Google Sheets / School software / Mixed)*
13. How many working days does it take from the last exam to report cards in parents' hands? *(1–3 / 4–7 / 8–14 / over 14)*
14. Does the school publish class positions? *(Yes, all classes / Only some classes / No)*

**Technology**

15. Which of these does the school use software for? *(Fees / Results / Attendance / Parent communication / None; tick all)*
16. What is the biggest barrier to using school software? *(Cost / Internet or power / Staff skills / Not convinced it's needed / Tried one and it didn't fit / Other)*
17. How does the school mainly communicate with parents? *(WhatsApp group / Individual WhatsApp / SMS / Email / Letters home / App)*
18. How often does a power or internet outage stop school administration? *(Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Rarely)*

**Optional**

19. Would you like a free copy of the report? If so, your email. *(Used only to send the report.)*

## Analysis plan

- Headline numbers: fee collection by week 3, hours spent chasing fees, days to deliver results, the cost bands.
- Splits: by state (for states with 10+ responses), by school size and by results method (Excel vs software).
- Charts for each headline, a methods note, and a downloadable summary PDF.
