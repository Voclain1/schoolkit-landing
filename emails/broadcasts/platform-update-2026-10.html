Here's what's new in SchoolKit this month.

1. Result Checker with PINs

Release results to every parent free of charge, or lock them behind result PINs. SchoolKit generates the PIN cards for you to print and hand out. Parents enter their PIN on your school's result checker page and see their child's result straight away.

2. Question bank and exam papers

Build a question bank for each subject. AI can draft the questions, and a teacher approves each one before it can be used. You can then set a paper from the bank, shuffle it into versions A to D, and export it as PDF, Word or CSV.

3. Complete report cards

Report cards now show attendance and promotion status, and the school chooses whether to show class positions. Marks can be out of any total, and cumulative results add up across the terms automatically.

4. Homework and absence alerts

Teachers can post homework that parents see on the parent app and portal, and families can reply. Parents also get a notification when their child is marked absent, so they don't have to wait for a phone call.

Coming soon: online exams on your school's lab computers, which keep working when the internet drops. We'll set them up with each school ahead of its exam period.

An update to our plans
