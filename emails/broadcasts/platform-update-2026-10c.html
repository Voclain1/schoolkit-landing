A clearer SchoolKit, and your school's own web address

Hello {{{FIRST_NAME|there}}},

Here is what has changed in SchoolKit this past week. First, one date for your diary.

Planned maintenance

SchoolKit may be briefly unavailable on Monday 26 October, 04:00–05:00 (Lagos time), while we move to faster servers. Nothing changes on your side: same address, same logins, same data.

1. A clearer, calmer dashboard

SchoolKit has a new look on the web and in the app. Figures sit on clean white cards, the text is easier to read, and the menu shows plainly which page you are on. On phones, the dashboard figures now fit their cards, however large your numbers grow. Until your school is fully set up, the dashboard also shows a checklist of what is left, with a button that takes you straight to each step.

2. Your school's own web address

Every school now has its own address, such as yourschool.schoolkit.ng. It opens your school's front page, with your news, photos, upcoming events, online admissions and the result checker. Parents sign in at /parents and staff at /staff on the same address. The pages are built for Google: in Settings → School website, under How you appear on Google, add your town and a short description, and see how your school will look in search results.

3. Add students from a photo of your register (beta)

Take a clear photo of one page of your class register, handwritten or printed, and SchoolKit turns it into a table of students. Nothing is saved until you have checked every row against the paper. It is now switched on for every school: on Students, press the small arrow beside Add student, then Scan a student list. It is in beta and can make mistakes, so tell us how it does for you. Importing from a spreadsheet (CSV) works as before.

4. Payment confirmations in your school's name

When a parent pays fees online, they now get a confirmation email from your school, showing the amount, the child, the term and the balance left.

Also new

A new look for your school's website, with Apply and Sign in always in reach. News and photos for the website can be posted from the staff app. Settings → Data protection has a ready-made letter asking parents' consent, and records when your school confirmed SchoolKit's terms. Staff, parents and students can ask for their account to be deleted from the app, and you decide what happens.

Stuck setting up, or adding students?

We want to hear about it. If anything has slowed you down, from creating classes and terms to bringing in your students, reply to this email and tell us:

  • which step you were on, and what happened;
  • how your student list is kept today: a spreadsheet, a register book, or another system;
  • roughly how many students you have.

We read every reply, and we can set things up with you on a call or bring your list in for you.
