SchoolKit now runs the whole school

Hello {{{FIRST_NAME|there}}},

Since our last update we've added the parts of school life that usually live in exercise books, files and WhatsApp groups. Here are the main ones.

1. Student and staff ID cards

Upload passport photos one by one or all at once, then print ID cards on A4 sheets or a PVC card printer. Each card has a QR code that anyone can scan with a phone camera to see whether the card is genuine and current. Date of birth, home address and parents' phone numbers are never printed.

2. The school store and asset register

Keep stock of uniforms, textbooks and stationery, size by size. When you sell to a child, the amount goes straight onto their bill. Stock takes, low-stock alerts and a register of your computers, projectors and furniture are included, along with a new Storekeeper role.

3. Online admissions

Families apply on your school's own admissions page and upload their documents. You review each application, record the entrance assessment and send an offer. When a family accepts, the child is admitted without anyone typing their details again.

4. Private messages and emails to parents

Parents can message the school privately from the parent app or portal, and your staff answer from one shared inbox. Announcements can now go out by email as well. Events can ask parents to reply "attending" or "not attending", so you know the numbers.

5. Homework handed in and marked online

Students hand in typed answers or photos of their work from the student app. Multiple-choice questions mark themselves, and marks can go straight into the gradebook. Optionally, AI can draft feedback for the teacher to edit. It's off until your school switches it on, and nothing reaches a student until the teacher approves it.

6. Boarding, transport, the library and the sickbay

Hostel bed lists, exeat requests from parents and visitor logs. Bus routes with a daily pick-up list. A library catalogue with lending, fines and reading resources for students. Health records and a sickbay log that tells parents when their child was seen. Hostel, bus and library charges go onto the child's bill.

Also new

Behaviour points and a discipline process with notices to parents. Reports you can download in Excel, PDF or CSV. An early warning for families likely to fall behind on fees. Staff salaries saved once and filled in on every payroll. A simpler menu, grouped into sections such as Students, Staff, Academics and Bursary.

Everything is in the menu when you sign in. Our Getting started guide, inside SchoolKit, walks through each feature step by step.
